History

3,000 years in one minute

Jews are not newcomers to this land. They are the people whose name, language and faith were born there.

  1. c. 1208 BCE

    An Egyptian pharaoh's victory stone names "Israel" as a people in Canaan, the oldest known mention. Petrie, Merneptah Stele

  2. 70 CE

    Rome destroys Jerusalem and the Second Temple. Josephus

  3. c. 135 CE

    After crushing a Jewish revolt, Rome renames Judaea "Syria Palaestina" and bars Jews from Jerusalem. "Palestine" was a name imposed by an empire. Britannica

  4. c. 200–400 CE

    Jews stay. The Mishnah and the Jerusalem Talmud are written in the Galilee. Encyclopaedia Judaica

  5. 1922

    The League of Nations recognizes "the historical connection of the Jewish people with Palestine" and the grounds for "reconstituting their national home." Mandate text

  6. 1947

    The UN offers two states. The Jewish Agency accepts. The Arab Higher Committee rejects, and Arab armies invade in 1948. UN Res. 181

  7. 1948–1972

    About 820,000 Jews leave or are driven from Arab countries. Most rebuild in Israel. JVL

  8. 2000 & 2008

    Israel offers a Palestinian state twice more. No deal. Abbas later admits he rejected the 2008 offer. AP

  9. 2005

    Israel leaves Gaza entirely: 21 settlements, about 8,500 to 9,000 settlers, every soldier. Britannica

  10. 2007

    Hamas seizes Gaza by force. "Hamas routed Fatah's militias in a week of fighting." CFR

  11. 7 Oct 2023

    Hamas invades Israel, murders 695 Israeli civilians, 71 foreigners and 373 security personnel, and takes 251 hostages. Israel's social security data (AFP) · ABC

The moral case

Judge each side by what it wants and what it does

Who targets civilians?

Intent separates war from atrocity.

HamasIts charter calls for "killing the Jews." On October 7 it murdered 695 Israeli civilians, including 36 children. The youngest was 10 months old. Charter · AFP
IsraelIt issues evacuation orders before operations. Even UNRWA's chief says his Gaza staff left after Israeli evacuation orders in October 2023. Times of Israel

Who lives with the other?

One side has two million Arab citizens. The other wants no Jews at all.

Israel21% of its people are Arab. 13 Arab members sit in the Knesset. A Muslim justice sits on the Supreme Court. CBS · Globes · Haaretz
HamasIt rejects "any alternative to the full and complete liberation of Palestine, from the river to the sea." Gaza has not held a legislative vote since 2006. Hamas 2017 · CFR

Who wanted two states?

Israel's founders accepted partition in 1947. Arab leaders rejected it and went to war. The pattern repeated in 1937, 2000 and 2008. Peace has always required one thing the other side refused: accepting a Jewish state.

The double standard

In 2018 the UN General Assembly could not pass a resolution simply condemning Hamas. It got 87 votes for and 58 against, and failed only because a two-thirds rule was imposed first. DW

If Hamas laid down its weapons tomorrow, the war would end. If Israel laid down its weapons tomorrow, there would be no Israel.

Answers

What you'll hear, and what to say

2029earliest court ruling

"Israel is committing genocide."

FALSE

No court has found genocide. Genocide means intent to destroy a people. Israel's war aim is to defeat Hamas.

  • The world court's case is still in written pleadings; Israel's final filing is due 22 May 2029. ICJ
  • The court's president at the time: the "plausible genocide" headline "isn't what the court decided." Judge Donoghue
  • "86% of genocide scholars": only 28% of the association's roughly 500 members voted. IAGS

If they say "the UN said so": a Human Rights Council panel said so. It is not a court, and the UN's own court has not ruled.

695Israeli civilians murdered

"October 7 was resistance," or "Israel killed its own people."

FALSE

Hamas went house to house murdering civilians. That is a massacre, not resistance.

  • Israel's social security data: 695 Israeli civilians killed, including 36 children; the youngest was 10 months old. AFP
  • The IDF's own probe found friendly fire only "in a handful of cases." Times of Israel
  • 251 people were dragged into Gaza; 85 came home in coffins. ABC

If they say "it didn't happen in a vacuum": 53% of Palestinians still called the attack "correct" in October 2025. PCPSR

Art. 7"killing the Jews"

"Hamas is a resistance movement."

FALSE

Hamas's charter calls for killing Jews, and its leaders promise to repeat October 7.

  • Charter, Article 7: "The Day of Judgement will not come about until Moslems fight the Jews (killing the Jews)." Yale Avalon
  • Ghazi Hamad, Hamas politburo, 24 Oct 2023: "we will do it twice and three times." MEMRI translation
  • Iran funds it: "up to $100 million annually" to Hamas and allied terror groups. US State Dept

If they say "Hamas was elected": once, in 2006, with 44%. No legislative vote since. CFR

10%of Palestinians want one equal state

"From the river to the sea just means equality."

FALSE

The river is the Jordan and the sea is the Mediterranean. In between is all of Israel.

  • Hamas, 2017: it "rejects any alternative to the full and complete liberation of Palestine, from the river to the sea." Hamas document
  • Only 10% of Palestinians choose one shared state. PCPSR, Aug 2026
  • Of US students backing the chant, 47% could name the river and sea; after basic facts, 67.8% dropped it. Hassner, WSJ
4offers, no acceptance

"Israel never wanted peace."

FALSE

Israel accepted partition in 1947 and offered statehood again in 2000 and 2008. Palestinian leaders said no.

  • 1947: the Jewish Agency accepted the UN plan; the Arab Higher Committee rejected it. UN
  • 2000: the Clinton Parameters offered 94 to 96% of the West Bank plus land swaps. The Second Intifada followed. US Embassy
  • 2008: Abbas admitted he rejected Olmert's offer. AP
0Israelis in Gaza, 2005–2023

"Gaza was an open-air prison under occupation."

FALSE

Israel left Gaza in 2005. Hamas took over in 2007 and chose tunnels and rockets over building a state.

  • 2005: all 21 settlements removed, about 8,500 to 9,000 settlers and every soldier. Britannica
  • 2007: "Hamas routed Fatah's militias in a week of fighting." CFR
  • World court judge Cleveland: Israel "did not exercise effective control over most of the day-to-day government administration" of Gaza. Hamas did. ICJ
48%of Israeli Jews are Mizrahi or Sephardi

"Jews are white European colonizers."

FALSE

You cannot colonize your own homeland. Half of Israel's Jews come from the Middle East and North Africa.

  • Israeli Jews: 48% Mizrahi or Sephardi, 45% Ashkenazi. Pew
  • About 850,000 Jews lived in Arab countries in 1948; 820,000 had left by 1972. JVL
  • Genetics "trace the origins of most Jewish Diaspora communities to the Levant." Nature, 2010

Under apartheid, Black South Africans could not vote. Israel's Arab citizens vote, sit in the Knesset and serve on its Supreme Court.

  • Israel is 21.1% Arab. Israel CBS
  • 13 of 120 Knesset members are Arab. Globes
  • Khaled Kabub became Israel's first permanent Muslim Supreme Court justice in 2022. Haaretz
Homeswhere hostages were held

"Hamas uses human shields."

TRUE

Hamas hid hostages among families and says protecting Gaza's civilians isn't its job.

  • Freed hostages were held "in private residential buildings with Palestinian families," even the UN reports. UN record
  • Hamas's Moussa Abu Marzouk: the tunnels are for fighters, not civilians. Times of Israel
87%of UN aid trucks intercepted

"Israel is starving Gaza on purpose."

MISLEADING

Aid went in and was intercepted before reaching people. The famine finding admits it never measured deaths.

  • The famine report itself: 87% of UN trucks "reportedly intercepted" en route. IPC
  • Its data: phone surveys and arm-band screenings. Mortality was inferred, not measured. IPC
  • Israel's aid authority: crossings reopened 19 May 2025, with no ban on UN aid. COGAT

If they say "Israel closed the crossings in 2025": it paused them for about eleven weeks, then reopened them on 19 May.

63%of US religious hate crimes

"Anti-Zionism isn't antisemitism."

FALSE

Denying Jews alone a state, and calling them Nazis for defending it, is antisemitism. The hate-crime numbers show where it leads.

  • 2025: 1,528 of 2,408 religion-based hate crimes in the US targeted Jews. FBI data via ADL
  • The international definition lists "comparisons of contemporary Israeli policy to that of the Nazis" as antisemitism. US State Dept
  • Criticism of Israel "similar to that leveled against any other country" is fair. A standard applied only to the Jewish state is not. IHRA
